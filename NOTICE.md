# Data & attribution

Kiwami's dictionary content is built from third-party data sources, distributed under their own licenses (separate from this project's code license):

- **[JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html)** (JMdict_e), from the [Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group](https://www.edrdg.org/) — licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The core headword/reading/gloss/kanji data.
- **[KANJIDIC2](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project)**, also from the [Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group](https://www.edrdg.org/) — licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Fetched by `scripts/fetch-kanjidic.sh` and parsed by `scripts/build-kanji.mjs` into the `kanji` table: the on'yomi/kun'yomi readings, English meanings, stroke count, classical radical number, and grade/JLPT/frequency shown in each result's per-character kanji breakdown. Its ~13,100 characters also define the character set for the `kanji_compounds` table (`scripts/build-compounds.mjs`).
- **[Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org/)** example sentences — licensed under [CC BY 2.0 FR](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/). Shown in each result's "Examples" panel.
- **[kuromoji.js](https://github.com/takuyaa/kuromoji.js)** and its bundled IPADIC dictionary data — licensed under Apache 2.0 / LGPL. Used in two places: (1) at build time (`scripts/build-furigana.mjs`) to tokenize every Tatoeba sentence and assign the furigana readings shown in the Examples panel; (2) at runtime (`src/dictionary/tokenizer.js`, via `deconjugate.js`) as a fallback when a user searches a conjugated form (e.g. "食べた") that the hand-rolled suffix rules can't resolve - it tokenizes the query and offers its own guess at the dictionary (base) form, covering irregulars (する/来る) and longer auxiliary chains the rules don't. Not used for the "Conjugate" button - that's the opposite direction (known entry -> conjugated forms), handled by jconj-js below.
- **[jconj-js](https://github.com/cobysy/jconj-js)** — a TS/JS table-based Japanese word conjugator by cobysy, licensed under MIT (Copyright (c) 2018 cobysy). This is the direct source of both `src/dictionary/lib/jconj.js` (its conjugation code, ported and trimmed for this app's "Conjugate" button, which generates a result's negative/polite/te-form/past/potential/passive/causative forms from its dictionary entry) and `src/dictionary/lib/jconj-tables.json` (generated by its `gulp build-conjtables`). Its own upstream chain:
  - jconj-js is a port of **[yamagoya/jconj](https://github.com/yamagoya/jconj)** (`conj.py`, Copyright (c) 2014, 2018 Stuart McGraw) — a standalone Python program demonstrating how the JMdictDB conjugation tables can be used independently of the rest of JMdictDB. It is not itself part of JMdictDB, and the repository carries a copyright notice but no license file.
  - The table data in `jconj-tables.json` originates in **[JMdictDB](https://gitlab.com/yamagoya/jmdictdb)** (Stuart McGraw), whose package is licensed under GPL-2.0+. jconj's `data/*.csv` are documented there as exact copies of JMdictDB's `conj.csv`, `conjo.csv`, `conjo_notes.csv`, `conotes.csv`, and `kwpos.csv` as of JMdictDB revision hg-20180525-61238f.
