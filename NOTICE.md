# Data & attribution

Kiwami's dictionary content is built from third-party data sources, distributed under their own licenses (separate from this project's code license):

- **[JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html)** (JMdict_e), from the [Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group](https://www.edrdg.org/) — licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The core headword/reading/gloss/kanji data.
- **[Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org/)** example sentences — licensed under [CC BY 2.0 FR](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/). Shown in each result's "Examples" panel.
- **[kuromoji.js](https://github.com/takuyaa/kuromoji.js)** and its bundled IPADIC dictionary data — licensed under Apache 2.0 / LGPL. Used in two places: (1) at build time (`scripts/build-furigana.mjs`) to tokenize every Tatoeba sentence and assign the furigana readings shown in the Examples panel; (2) at runtime (`src/dictionary/tokenizer.js`, via `deconjugate.js`) as a fallback when a user searches a conjugated form (e.g. "食べた") that the hand-rolled suffix rules can't resolve - it tokenizes the query and offers its own guess at the dictionary (base) form, covering irregulars (する/来る) and longer auxiliary chains the rules don't. Not used for the "Conjugate" button - that's the opposite direction (known entry -> conjugated forms), handled by jconj-js below.
- **[jconj-js](https://github.com/cobysy/jconj-js)** (`src/dictionary/lib/jconj.js`, `jconj-tables.json`) — a JS/TS port of the JMdictDB project's table-based verb/adjective conjugator ([yamagoya/jconj](https://github.com/yamagoya/jconj)) — licensed under MIT. Ported and trimmed for this app's "Conjugate" button, which generates a result's negative/polite/te-form/past/potential/passive/causative forms from its dictionary entry.
